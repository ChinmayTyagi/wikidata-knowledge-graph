Wikidata Reading Graph

Your Wikipedia reading history, mapped onto Wikidata's knowledge graph. Nodes are articles you read; edges connect articles that share a structural trait in Wikidata (occupation, instance-of, genre, and so on). Hover an edge to see why two articles are connected.

Built from a personal Wikipedia browsing history export and the Wikidata knowledge graph. Edge weight = inverse document frequency of shared (property, value) pairs — rarer shared traits pull nodes closer.
